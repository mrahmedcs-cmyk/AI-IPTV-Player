AI IPTV Player

Your playlists.

AI IPTV Player is a modern media player for Android TV and Amazon Fire TV. Explore movies, series and live channels from your own playlists with an interface designed for television remote controls.

Cinematic browsing

Poster-focused browsing for movies and series.

Made for TV

Remote-friendly navigation and playback controls.

Enhanced metadata

Optional artwork and information integration for movies and shows.

AI IPTV Player does not host or distribute media, channels or playlists. Users are responsible for providing and using their own legally authorized content. Third-party artwork and metadata belong to their respective owners.
